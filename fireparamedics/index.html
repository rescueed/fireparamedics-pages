The 911 Problem

When you call 911 for a fire or medical emergency, your call goes to police dispatch first. They then transfer you to fire dispatch, wasting precious seconds when every moment counts.

In a fire or medical emergency, seconds save lives. The average 911 call takes 2-3 minutes just to reach the right dispatcher. For cardiac arrest, brain damage starts in 4-6 minutes. For fires, a room can be fully engulfed in 3-5 minutes.

⚠️ Critical Fact: Most fire departments are separate from police departments. When you call 911, you're talking to police first, then being transferred. This delay can cost lives.

The Solution

Fire/Paramedics gives you direct access to fire department dispatch centers, bypassing 911 police dispatch entirely.

With one tap, you connect directly to:

  • Fire Department Dispatch
  • Paramedic/EMS Dispatch
  • Emergency Medical Services

No transfers. No delays. Direct connection to help.

📍 Enable Location Services

The app needs your location to find the nearest fire department. Please enable location services:

iPhone

  1. Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services
  2. Make sure "Location Services" is ON
  3. Scroll down to "Safari"
  4. Select "While Using the App"

Android

  1. Settings → Location
  2. Make sure Location is ON
  3. Go to Apps → Chrome
  4. Permissions → Location → "Allow only while using the app"
